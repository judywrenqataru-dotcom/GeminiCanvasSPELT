How to build

Four steps from word list to shareable app

Each step has a short screencast. Watch it, then try the step yourself.

Step 1 · Plan

Let Gemini Chat write your Canvas prompt

Don't write the app prompt yourself. Tell Gemini about your class and attach your material, then ask it to write a prompt for Canvas.

  • Who your learners are
  • Attach any context documents or models
  • Describe what the app should do
  • Give guardrails
  • Phone, laptop or projector
Shortcut: use the prompt builder below
Step 2 · Build

Attach, turn on Canvas, paste

Start a new chat, attach the same material, switch on Canvas and paste the prompt Gemini wrote for you.

On a laptop
  1. Go to gemini.google.com
  2. + → Upload or Add from Drive
  3. Tools → Canvas
  4. Paste your prompt, press Enter
On a phone
  1. Open the Gemini app
  2. + → attach a file or photo
  3. Tools → Canvas
  4. Paste, then tap Preview

Canvas sits under Tools or More tools, depending on your version.

Step 3 · Test & refine

Play like a student, fix one thing at a time

Switch to Preview and play the whole game. Then ask for one change per message, so you can see what each change did.

  • Are the answers correct?
  • Does the English fit the level?
  • Does it work on a phone?
Stuck? See the fix-it phrases below
Step 4 · Share

Share one link that works on any device

  1. Click Share (top right of the Canvas panel)
  2. Copy the public link
  3. Post it for students, or turn it into a QR code
  4. Students open the link, tap Continue and play

About public links

  • Anyone with the link can open your app and make their own copy.
  • Don't ask students to type names or personal details into an app.
  • You can delete a link any time in Gemini (Settings → Your public links).
Ideas

Ways to use review apps in class

One app can do many jobs. These work in large classes and with few devices.

Getting the link to students

QR code

Put it on a slide or print it on the board. In Chrome, right-click the page → Create QR code.

WhatsApp or class group

Paste the link with one line: what to do and by when.

Google Classroom or LMS

Add the link as material, so students can find it again before the test.
